Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between Free and Pro?
Free includes all core features like Influencer Sniper, Wallet Stalker, and basic analytics. Pro adds advanced multi-wallet management, custom strategies, whale predictions, priority speed, and private alpha signals.
Is this secure? Do you access my wallet?
100% secure. Beacon runs entirely in your browser and never asks for private keys. It helps you execute trades faster on axiom.trade, but you approve every transaction.
How do I install the bookmarklet?
Simply drag the "Activate Beacon" button from the hero section to your browser's bookmarks bar. Then visit axiom.trade and click the bookmark to activate. If you don't see the bookmarks bar, press Ctrl+Shift+B (Cmd+Shift+B on Mac).
Can I really beat other bots?
Beacon gives you a massive advantage through automation, signal detection, and MEV protection. Pro users get even more advantages with whale predictions and priority transaction speed.
